# Aurora Valley Community Hospital

Aurora Valley Community Hospital (AVCH) is a fictional, not-for-profit general
hospital located at **1450 Willow Park Avenue, Brookhaven, Massachusetts
02468**. Its main telephone number is **(555) 014-2080**.

The hospital has 180 licensed inpatient beds, a 24-hour emergency department,
six operating rooms, and an outpatient diagnostics center. AVCH serves
Brookhaven and the surrounding Aurora Valley communities.

Visitor information desk hours are 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. daily. Patients and
visitors should use the main entrance at Willow Park Avenue unless the care team
instructs them to use the emergency entrance.

This document describes a fictional organization for product testing only. It
does not represent a real hospital, address, telephone number, or service.
