# Hypertension

Hypertension is persistently high blood pressure. It often has no symptoms, but
over time it can damage the heart, brain, kidneys, and blood vessels.

Common symptoms:
- Most patients have no obvious symptoms
- Some may report headaches, dizziness, or blurred vision, but these are not
  specific

Common causes and risk factors:
- Family history
- Older age
- High-salt diet
- Obesity
- Physical inactivity
- Chronic kidney disease
- Stress
- Alcohol use
- Certain medications or endocrine disorders

How hypertension is commonly treated:
- Regular blood pressure monitoring
- Reduced sodium intake
- Exercise and weight management
- Limiting alcohol
- Stress reduction
- Blood pressure medicines such as ACE inhibitors, ARBs, calcium channel
  blockers, or diuretics

Immediate evaluation is important for severely elevated blood pressure with
chest pain, neurologic symptoms, severe shortness of breath, or vision changes.
