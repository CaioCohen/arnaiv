# Arthritis

Arthritis refers to joint inflammation and includes several conditions such as
osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and other inflammatory joint disorders.

Common symptoms:
- Joint pain
- Stiffness
- Swelling
- Reduced range of motion
- Warmth or tenderness around a joint

Common causes and risk factors:
- Age-related joint wear in osteoarthritis
- Autoimmune disease in rheumatoid arthritis
- Prior joint injury
- Family history
- Obesity, which can increase joint stress

How arthritis is commonly treated:
- Activity modification and physical therapy
- Weight management when appropriate
- Pain relief medicines
- Anti-inflammatory treatment
- Joint protection strategies
- Referral for rheumatology or orthopedic follow-up when needed

Urgent medical review is important for a hot swollen joint with fever, sudden
inability to use a limb, or severe rapidly worsening pain.
