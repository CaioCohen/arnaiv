# Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection of the lungs that causes inflammation of the air
sacs. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or other organisms.

Common symptoms:
- Fever or chills
- Cough, often with mucus
- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain that worsens with breathing or coughing
- Fatigue
- Confusion in older adults

Common causes and risk factors:
- Bacterial infection
- Viral infection
- Older age or very young age
- Chronic lung disease
- Weakened immune system
- Aspiration in some patients

How pneumonia is commonly treated:
- Antibiotics for bacterial pneumonia
- Supportive care such as rest, fluids, fever control, and oxygen if needed
- Antiviral treatment in selected cases
- Hospital care for low oxygen, dehydration, or severe illness

Urgent evaluation is important for difficulty breathing, low oxygen signs,
confusion, high fever with deterioration, or inability to keep fluids down.
