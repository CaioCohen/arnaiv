# Gastrointestinal Conditions

Aurora Valley Community Hospital treats common gastrointestinal conditions such
as gastritis, gastroenteritis, acid reflux, constipation, diarrhea, mild
dehydration, and some non-surgical abdominal pain presentations. This category
does not include every digestive disease.

Common symptoms:
- Abdominal pain or cramping
- Nausea or vomiting
- Diarrhea or constipation
- Bloating
- Heartburn
- Reduced appetite
- Mild dehydration symptoms

Common causes and risk factors:
- Viral or bacterial infections
- Dietary triggers
- Medication side effects
- Acid reflux
- Inflammation of the stomach lining
- Dehydration
- Stress-related symptom worsening in some patients

How these conditions are commonly treated:
- Oral or intravenous fluids depending on severity
- Diet adjustments
- Antacid or acid-suppressing medicines when appropriate
- Anti-nausea medicines
- Observation and reassessment of abdominal symptoms
- Referral for imaging or specialist evaluation when needed

Urgent evaluation is needed for severe abdominal pain, repeated vomiting,
bloody stool, black stool, signs of severe dehydration, or abdominal symptoms
with fainting or confusion.
