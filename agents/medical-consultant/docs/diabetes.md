# Diabetes

Diabetes is a condition in which the body has trouble controlling blood sugar.
The main forms are type 1 diabetes, type 2 diabetes, and gestational diabetes.

Common symptoms:
- Increased thirst
- Frequent urination
- Fatigue
- Blurry vision
- Unexplained weight loss
- Slow-healing wounds
- Recurrent infections

Common causes and risk factors:
- Autoimmune destruction of insulin-producing cells in type 1 diabetes
- Insulin resistance in type 2 diabetes
- Family history
- Overweight or obesity
- Physical inactivity
- Pregnancy-related hormonal changes in gestational diabetes

How diabetes is commonly treated:
- Monitoring blood glucose
- Nutrition changes and exercise
- Oral medicines for many type 2 patients
- Insulin for type 1 diabetes and some type 2 or gestational cases
- Ongoing monitoring for kidney, eye, nerve, and cardiovascular complications

Urgent assessment is needed for symptoms of very high or very low blood sugar,
including confusion, vomiting, dehydration, severe weakness, or loss of
consciousness.
