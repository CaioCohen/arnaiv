# Uncomplicated Fractures

An uncomplicated fracture is a broken bone without major complications such as
open wounds, severe displacement, or major nerve and blood vessel injury.

Common symptoms:
- Pain at the injury site
- Swelling
- Bruising
- Deformity in some cases
- Inability or difficulty using the affected limb

Common causes and risk factors:
- Falls
- Sports injuries
- Direct trauma
- Overuse in some stress fractures
- Osteoporosis or weakened bone structure

How uncomplicated fractures are commonly treated:
- Immobilization with a splint, cast, or brace
- Pain management
- X-ray confirmation
- Orthopedic follow-up
- Surgery only when alignment or healing concerns require it

Emergency evaluation is needed for open fractures, loss of circulation,
numbness, major deformity, uncontrolled pain, or injuries involving the head,
neck, spine, or hip.
