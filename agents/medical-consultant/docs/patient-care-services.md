# Aurora Valley Community Hospital — patient care services

Aurora Valley Community Hospital is a fictional general hospital for testing
purposes. It provides emergency, inpatient, outpatient, and referral services
for adults and children.

The emergency department evaluates urgent conditions such as chest pain,
breathing difficulty, severe allergic reactions, serious injuries, stroke-like
symptoms, severe infections, and acute abdominal pain. It stabilizes patients
and transfers highly specialized cases when needed.

Its medical and surgical services commonly care for people with asthma and
chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), diabetes, hypertension, heart
failure, pneumonia, urinary tract infections, gastrointestinal conditions,
arthritis, uncomplicated fractures, and recovery needs after general surgery.

AVCH has outpatient clinics for primary care, cardiology, endocrinology,
pulmonology, orthopedics, women's health, behavioral health, and pediatric
care. It does not provide transplant surgery, major burn-center services,
high-risk neonatal intensive care, or complex pediatric cardiac surgery. Those
patients are referred to regional specialty centers.
