# Aurora Valley Community Hospital — employee time-off policy

This is a fictional policy for testing purposes only.

Full-time employees accrue **20 paid time off (PTO) days** during each completed
calendar year. PTO accrues monthly and may be used after the employee completes
90 days of employment. Up to five unused PTO days may carry into the following
calendar year; any additional unused PTO expires on December 31.

Employees receive **10 paid sick days** per calendar year. Sick days do not
carry over. Employees should notify their supervisor at least one hour before a
scheduled shift when possible. A medical note may be requested for an absence
of three or more consecutive scheduled workdays.

The hospital observes these paid holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King
Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day
after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. Employees required to work on an
observed holiday receive holiday premium pay according to their employment
agreement.

PTO requests of three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least 30
days in advance. Approval depends on staffing needs and is not guaranteed until
confirmed by the department manager.
